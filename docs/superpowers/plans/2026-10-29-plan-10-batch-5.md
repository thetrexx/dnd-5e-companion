# Plan 10, Batch 5: The rest of SCAG, the DMG races and the Giants feats Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Finish the "more books" project. Add:
- SCAG's last 5 subclasses: Path of the Battlerager, Arcana Domain, Purple Dragon Knight, Way of the Long Death and The Undying;
- the Elk and Tiger totem options;
- the SCAG subraces and variants, the Svirfneblin Magic feat, and the DMG's Aasimar and Eladrin;
- the 8 feats from Glory of the Giants.

**Architecture:**
- **Content:** almost all of it is data in `data/*.json`, written from the page images. No new spells: all 53 spells the new records name already exist.
- **One code change, for tiefling variants:** a subrace flag, `replacesSpells`. A flagged subrace's spell grants replace its parent race's grants instead of adding to them.
- **Half-elf and tiefling variants are subrace records.** They use the existing Subrace picker, so the editor gains nothing new. Variants that are only passive or flavour get no record, since the app would show nothing different for them.
- **Giant feats** reuse feat-owned option groups (like Fighting Initiate). Strike of the Giants owns a pick of 6 strikes. Rune Shaper owns a level-scaled pick of 13 runes, and each rune grants its spell.

**Tech Stack:** Preact + TypeScript + Vite, Vitest, Playwright, Node 24 scripts with poppler.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-more-books-design.md`, section 4 (batch 5) and section 5. It extends `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-dnd-cheatsheet-design.md` (the base spec).

**Inventory:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-29-plan-10-batch-5-inventory.md`. It was read off the page images on 2026-10-29. It gives names, levels, pages, costs, numbers, the ids to use and collisions, with no rules text. Use it to find things, but the page image decides every fact on a card.

## Global Constraints

The Global Constraints of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-plan-5-batch-1-bg3-parity.md` apply unchanged:
- own words only: no run of 8+ words may match the page (`check:ocr` enforces this), and never write out rules text in files, messages or reports;
- facts come from the page image, never from memory;
- book ids and card labels (`Full rules: SCAG p. N`, `DMG p. N`, `Giants p. N`);
- ids never change;
- the summons rule;
- size stays ≤ 450 KB gzipped (240.7 KB before this batch).

The batch 2 rules on the in-scope feat rule and on bonus-spell modelling also apply (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-plan-6-batch-2-xanathars.md`). One of them: passive feats and passive traits stay out, and the report names what it left out.

Also:
- **Pages:** `npm run extract:pages -- <from> <to> <book>`, with book `scag` (PDF = book + 1), `dmg` (PDF = book + 4) or `bgg` (PDF = book). The pages from the inventory are already in `.cache/<book>/` (git-ignored; re-extract them if missing). bgg has no text layer, so its cards are checked by eye against the image, and `check:ocr` cannot check them.
- **Every new record carries its `book`:** `scag`, `dmg` or `bgg`. The one exception is the half-elf Mask of the Wild card in Task 1.
- **Never hand-edit `data/spells.json`.** No task changes spells.
- **Ids** are the ones in inventory section 7. The collisions are resolved as follows:
  - `way-of-the-long-death-touch-of-death`;
  - `purple-dragon-knight-bulwark`;
  - feat cards use the feat id (see Task 4), so `vigor-of-the-hill-giant` needs no `bulwark` id;
  - `rune-shaper-comprehend-languages` is not needed (Task 4 grants the spell on the feat).
- **Upcast and restricted race spells** say so in the grant's `uses`, in the style of the existing tiefling grant ("Once per long rest, cast as a 2nd-level spell"). Examples: "Once per long rest, enlarge only, on yourself only"; Hellfire's "Once per day" (the page's wording).
- **Missing ability:** where the page names no spellcasting ability (Eladrin Fey Step), leave `ability` off the grant. Misty step needs none.
- **Out of scope** (the report lists them): backgrounds (Giant Foundling, Rune Carver, the SCAG backgrounds: the app has no background records); the Skilled and Tough bonus feats (passive); the Feral and Appearance tiefling variants; the half-elf variants Keen Senses, Elf Weapon Training, Fleet of Foot and swim speed; Svirfneblin and Ghostwise traits (passive; the subrace records still exist so players can pick them); Potent Spellcasting, Royal Envoy, Undying Nature and Iron Stomach (passive or rest-only); the giant feats' passive parts, which become one description line on the feat; spiked armor as an item (a card line names the requirement).

2. **Tiefling variant swaps.** Each variant has its own spells and none of the parent's except where it says otherwise:
   - a Devil's Tongue tiefling 5 has vicious mockery, charm person and enthrall, and not thaumaturgy, hellish rebuke or darkness;
   - a Hellfire tiefling keeps thaumaturgy and darkness, and has burning hands instead of hellish rebuke;
   - a Winged tiefling has no race spells at all.
   Pinned in Task 1.
3. **Arcana Domain's spell picks.**
   - Arcane Initiate's 2 wizard cantrips don't count against cleric cantrips known.
   - Arcane Mastery offers four slots at Cleric 17 (one wizard spell each of levels 6, 7, 8 and 9). They are always prepared. A Cleric 16 sees them locked with "Unlocks at Cleric 17".
   Pinned in Task 3.
4. **Feat-owned picks scale with character level.** Rune Shaper gives 1 rune slot at total level 8, 2 at 9 and 3 at 17. Strike of the Giants gives 1 pick at any level, and the chosen strike's card shows on the character's actions. Pinned in Task 4.
5. **Race spells unlock by character level.** A Duergar 2 sees enlarge/reduce locked. At 3 it is unlocked, and its uses say enlarge only, self only. Pinned in Task 1.
