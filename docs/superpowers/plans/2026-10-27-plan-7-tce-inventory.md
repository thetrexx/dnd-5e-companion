Source: page images from `.cache/tce/p<N>.png` (book page numbers). Names, levels, pages, costs and short labels only.
Cost key: **A** = action, **BA** = bonus action, **R** = reaction, **P** = passive, **free** = no action (a trigger or rider), **rest** = only changes at a rest or level-up (rebuild-only).
Abbreviations: PB = proficiency bonus, SR/LR = short/long rest, SP = sorcery points, CD = Channel Divinity, MA = Martial Arts.

## 5. Custom Lineage and the origin rules (p7-8)

- **Customizing Your Origin (p7)**: rules, not a race. Three parts: Ability Score Increases (move racial ASIs to any abilities), Languages (swap), Proficiencies (swap; Proficiency Swaps table). Rebuild-only, nothing usable on a turn.
- **Custom Lineage (p8)**, a race option. Choices:
  - creature type humanoid; size **Small or Medium** (choice)
  - speed 30 ft
  - **+2 to one ability score** (choice)
  - **1 feat** of your choice (must qualify)
  - variable trait: **darkvision 60 ft OR proficiency in 1 skill** (choice)
  - languages: Common + 1 (choice)
  Note: the chosen feat is the only way it gains anything usable in play.
- Changing a Skill (p8) and Changing Your Subclass (p8): DM rules sidebars. These aren't player options for the app.

## Ambiguities and notes
- Blazing Revival (Wildfire 14) prints no action cost; it triggers when you drop to 0 HP. Vigilant Rebuke (Watchers 15) is a reaction with no use limit printed.
- Instinctive Pounce and Magical Inspiration modify existing PHB features, but the book doesn't say "replaces". Only these say REPLACES: Blessed Strikes (Divine Strike or Potent Spellcasting), Deft Explorer (Natural Explorer), Favored Foe (Favored Enemy), Primal Awareness (Primeval Awareness), Nature's Veil (Hide in Plain Sight), Primal Companion (Ranger's Companion).
- Fathomless and Genie Expanded Spell Lists add choices to the warlock list. Aberrant Mind and Clockwork Soul spells are known automatically. Domain, oath, circle and Fey Wanderer spells are always prepared or known.
- Battle Master Builds (p46-47) are advice, not new options.
- Page refs in the subclass and feature tables are where each feature's heading appears; when a feature crosses a page break, both pages are listed.
